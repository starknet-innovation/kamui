features:
  - title: Social login
    details: Users sign in with Google. Their account is a Starknet smart account owned by a key on their device. No seed phrase, no extension.
  - title: Session keys
    details: Ask once for exactly what your app needs (contracts, functions, spending limits, expiry). Then sign calls yourself, with no more prompts.
  - title: Sponsored transactions
    details: Your app pays the fees through Kamui, so users never need STRK to start playing.
  - title: One keychain
    details: Every app sends users to the same keychain page, so a user who signed in once is recognised everywhere.
